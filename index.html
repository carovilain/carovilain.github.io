Hey, I’m Caro.

I’m a visual storyteller & mobility advocate based in Santa Monica, CA. I spent most of the last decade working on marketing campaigns for clients including Paramount Studios, Netflix, DreamWorks, LA28, and Sony.

After an unpolished social media experiment of mine reached over 25M views, I realized the value of combining my video editing and storytelling background with my passion for safe streets. Living car-free in Los Angeles will do that to you.

That realization shaped my current focus: I collaborate with advocacy organizations like Streets For All and Move LA, while continuing to use my brand marketing experience to create content for companies whose missions align with livable cities, including Hayden AI, Lumos Helmets, Big Blue Bus, and Wombi.
