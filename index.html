About

Caro is a Visual Storyteller & Mobility Advocate based in Santa Monica, CA. Through her extensive knowledge of video editing and storytelling, she has worked on campaigns for clients including Paramount Studios, Netflix, DreamWorks, LA28, and Sony.

Living car-free in Los Angeles, she developed a passion for mobility advocacy and the many issues it intersects with, and she now uses her skills to amplify the messages of advocacy organizations such as Streets For All, Move LA, and others.
